Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Small Lanterns is an independent animation channel. This policy explains what information this website and the Small Lanterns publishing tool handle.

This website

  • This site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or sign-in.
  • It does not collect or store any personal information from visitors.
  • Links to YouTube take you to youtube.com, where Google's own privacy policy applies.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and reliability, under its own policy.

The Small Lanterns publishing tool (Google API access)

"Small Lanterns" is also the name of a private tool, registered with Google, that the channel owner uses to manage their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and is used by the channel owner only.

  • What it accesses: with the channel owner's permission, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions and to organize playlists on the Small Lanterns channel, and the YouTube Analytics API to read that channel's own performance reports.
  • How data is used: only to publish Small Lanterns films and to understand which films viewers enjoy. Analytics are aggregate channel reports and contain no information that identifies individual viewers.
  • Storage: the access token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Children

Small Lanterns films are not made for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be sent through the Small Lanterns YouTube channel.
